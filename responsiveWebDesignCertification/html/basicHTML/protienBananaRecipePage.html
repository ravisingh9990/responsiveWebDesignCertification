Protien Banana Shake Recipe

An amazing and tasty Protien Banana Shake recipe for fitness lovers

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Banana
  • Dates
  • Whey Protien

Instructions

  1. Pour 250 ml milk in the blender jar
  2. Put 3-4 Bananas in the blender jar
  3. Add 3 Dates and 1 Scoop of your Whey Protien
  4. Blend them togather for 20-30 Seconds
  5. Your shake is ready
